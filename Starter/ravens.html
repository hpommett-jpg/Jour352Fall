As of September 29, the Ravens are 2–1 after three regular-season games. They opened the season with a 41–23 win over Indianapolis, lost 24–17 to New Orleans, and then defeated Dallas 34–31 in Rio de Janeiro.

One of the coolest things about this season is that Baltimore played Dallas in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, on September 27. It was the Ravens' third international game and their first game in Rio. In a last second comeback, they won a game-winning 56-yard field goal by Tyler Loop.
