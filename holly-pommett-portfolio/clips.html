CP_HereAndNow: Breaking News Graphics

Graphic Design · Social Media

A set of Instagram graphics for CP_HereAndNow, a College Park news account, summarizing reporting on the three-month suspension of a local bar ordered by the Prince George's County Board of License Commissioners.

The posts pair campus and city photography with a clean, readable text card, so followers get the key facts and quotes at a glance before clicking through to the full story.
